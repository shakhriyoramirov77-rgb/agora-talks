We are living in the 21st century. Unlike any other time in history, we have a network that allows us to do whatever we want. We are more connected than ever.

Being connected is not the same as reading together. What we do is plainer than that. We choose a book worth the time, read it properly, and meet on Sunday to argue about it: the work itself in front of us, not a summary of it, not what a lecture said it meant.

Our aim is to keep that room open to anyone who wants it. Everything we read is in the public domain, so no one is kept out by the price of a copy, and nobody needs a faculty, a degree or an invitation to sit down. Come with an argument of your own and you belong at the table.

The only way to deal with an unfree world is to become so absolutely free that your very existence is an act of rebellion.

Albert Camus

We want to do the exact same thing. We are not silent! We will preserve and protect knowledge for coming generations.
